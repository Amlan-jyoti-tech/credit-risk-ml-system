# Phase 0.3R: Target Definition & Label Cleaning (Full Dataset)

## 1. Prediction Objective
The objective of this system is to accurately predict whether a loan will eventually **default** (or be charged off).

## 2. Prediction Point
The prediction point is **loan origination**.
The model must only use information legitimately available to Lending Club at the moment the loan decision was made.

> [!WARNING]
> **Data Leakage Prevention:** All post-origination features (e.g., `out_prncp`, `total_pymnt`, `recoveries`, `hardship_*`, `settlement_*`, `last_pymnt_d`) MUST be excluded from predictive features in Phase P0.4.

## 3. `loan_status` Distribution (Raw Dataset)
Before cleaning, the target column `loan_status` contained the following distribution across 2,260,701 rows:

- **Fully Paid:** 1,076,751
- **Current:** 878,317
- **Charged Off:** 268,559
- **Late (31-120 days):** 21,467
- **In Grace Period:** 8,436
- **Late (16-30 days):** 4,349
- **Does not meet the credit policy. Status:Fully Paid:** 1,988
- **Does not meet the credit policy. Status:Charged Off:** 761
- **Default:** 40

## 4. Target Mapping (`default_flag`)
We map definitive outcomes to a binary `default_flag` column (0/1).

**Non-Default (0):**
- `Fully Paid`
- `Does not meet the credit policy. Status:Fully Paid`

**Default (1):**
- `Charged Off`
- `Default`
- `Does not meet the credit policy. Status:Charged Off`

## 5. Excluded Statuses
The following statuses are explicitly excluded because their final outcome (Default vs. Fully Paid) is not yet determined:
- `Current`
- `Late (31-120 days)`
- `Late (16-30 days)`
- `In Grace Period`

> [!NOTE]
> Including "Late" statuses as defaults introduces label noise (some cure and pay off). Including "Current" loans as non-defaults heavily biases the model toward recent loans which haven't had time to default. The most rigorous approach for a static ML problem is to train only on definitive historical outcomes.

## 6. Dataset Maturity & Temporal Limitation
By excluding `Current` and late loans, we inherently filter out a large percentage of recent loans (e.g., 2017-2018) that simply haven't had time to finish their 36 or 60-month terms.

This means a naive temporal split (e.g., "test on 2018 data") will be evaluating on a skewed distribution of loans that either paid off exceptionally fast or defaulted exceptionally fast.

**Action Plan:** This temporal maturity limitation will be explicitly addressed in Phase P0.5/P0.6 when designing the train/validation/test splits.

## 7. Storage format
The cleaned dataset has dropped the `loan_status` column and generated the `default_flag` column. To improve storage efficiency and load times, it is saved as Parquet:
`data/processed/lending_club_target_cleaned.parquet`
